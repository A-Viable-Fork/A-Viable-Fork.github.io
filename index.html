Two readers, two paths

Reading as a human

Start with the argument, the-climb-of-synthesis.md, two pages, then walk one corpus room (eggs is the most visceral), then open the app and hold the feed.

Reading as (or with) an LLM

Point it at llms.txt, which orders the repository to prevent flattening, then let it read and synthesize, then rerun the checks yourself. Anything it asserts is recomputable against the public graph in minutes.
