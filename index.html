EpiStack

EpiStack is a protocol on which independently governed communities build knowledge on their own terms while composing with each other. The Knowledge Game is the first app on the platform: a feed you steer over claims you check.

The submission as six questions

1. What is the goal?What the competition asked for, read as one requirement: claims that still stand when trust in their producer is turned down to zero.

The competition asked for epistemic tooling on hard contested questions, its three named cases being pandemic origins, black holes, and eggs: turn messy sources into typed claims tied to their origins, make the relations among claims legible, tell a reader what to believe and what to look at next, scale with better AI, and compound across people and teams. This submission reads all of that as one requirement: a claim counts as knowledge when it still stands after trust in whoever produced it is turned down toward zero. Work compounds exactly when communities' claims compose without importing each other's authority.

2. What has been performing this function?The internet, carrying claims on a substrate that sees only packets: three configurations, encoded as kernels and priced by the conformance checker.

The internet, in three configurations, carrying claims on a substrate that sees only packets. A cooperative kernel where social accountability supplied the verification; the same contracts meeting an adversarial population; a patched kernel where authorities compensate for standing that does not recompute. Each era, encoded as a kernel and run through the protocol's conformance checker, violates exactly the invariants the contracts fix, with each violation mapped to a documented failure, so the recentralization is derived rather than asserted. The same trajectory is now beginning one layer up, over claims themselves.

3. Given recursive modelers, how does it need to be shaped?Fix the little that composition and adversaries require; leave every semantic judgment free.

The history above is what recursive modelers do to fixed trust signals: model them, aim at them, and manufacture them cheaper than the substance they once certified. The division that survives this: fix the little that composition and adversaries require, as contracts, and leave every semantic judgment free, as parameters, because semantic controls spend the exact variety communities need for judging.

The Contract Register The Asymmetric Weapon
4. What is the shape?Eleven fixed contracts and a gate; everything else is a free parameter.

Eleven fixed contracts and a gate. Claims are typed and content-addressed; a grade recomputes from the public graph; an import grounds nothing until a local fork types it; views move no grade. Everything else, the type systems, the standing rules, the forums, the licenses, is a free parameter, and a boundary linter holds the two registers disjoint.

The Contract Register The Math Kernel
5. What else has been shaped like this?Every mature institution, by hand. Nothing yet composes all five axes.

Every mature institution runs these mechanisms by hand: precedent chains in common law, dependency graphs in software, replication in science, load orders in game modding, slashing in decentralized finance. The lineage corpus grounds that record and one conjecture: nothing yet composes all five axes for empirical and contested knowledge.

The Lineage Corpus
6. What is the shape, running?This app: grades computed on your device, ranking set by you and moving no grade.

This app. A feed over typed claims, every grade computed on your device from the public graph, the ranking set by you and moving no grade. The repository's checks rerun in about two minutes, and the substrate is pinned to the protocol by commit and hash.

Two readers, two paths

With the frame above, here is how to read further.

Reading as a human

Start with the argument, the-climb-of-synthesis.md, two pages, then walk one corpus room (eggs is the most visceral), then open the app and hold the feed.

Reading as (or with) an LLM

Point it at llms.txt, which orders the repository to prevent flattening, then let it read and synthesize, then rerun the checks yourself. Anything it asserts is recomputable against the public graph in minutes.
